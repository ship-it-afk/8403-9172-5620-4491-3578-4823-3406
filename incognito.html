Local Protection

  • Authentication Shield

    FastBrowser doesn’t automatically sign you into Google, Ambient, or other site accounts. This prevents immediate session linking across the web.

  • Ephemeral Site Data

    Cookies and site data are kept temporarily to ensure sites function, but are wiped the moment your Private Window closes.

  • Persistent Downloads

    Any files you explicitly choose to download will remain on your device after the session ends for your convenience.

Note: While Private Window keeps your history off this device, you aren't invisible. Your ISP, employer, and the websites you visit (including Ambient services) can still see your network activity as outlined in our Privacy Policy.
